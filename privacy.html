Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

This notice covers the MIDAP project website and the code-release notification form, both run by CAP Lab at Seoul National University.

What we collect

We collect the email address you submit through the form. You may also add your GitHub username if you want to.

How we use it

We use this information to tell you when the source code is publicly released. We may send one follow-up message to correct the release link if needed. We never use your GitHub username to gather anything else from your profile.

Legal basis & storage

We process this information based on your consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time by asking us to delete your data. Google Forms collects and stores the submissions. Google may hold the data on servers in the United States or other countries. Only the project members who handle the release notification can see the responses.

Website analytics

To understand how many people find this project, we count visits with GoatCounter, an open-source analytics service. It sets no cookies and does not track you across websites. For each visit it records the page address, the referring page, and your browser, operating system, screen size and country. It does not store IP addresses, and none of this identifies you personally. We see only aggregate counts, never individual visitors.

You can opt out by blocking gc.zgo.at in your browser or ad blocker. The page works normally either way. We also use Google Search Console, which reports how often this page appears in Google search results; it adds no code to the page and gives us aggregate figures only.

Your choices & rights

Giving us your information is voluntary. Without an email address you will not receive the release notification. You can ask us to view, correct or delete your information at any time.

Contact
