Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

This notice covers the MIDAP code-release notification form run by CAP Lab at Seoul National University.

What we collect

We collect the email address you submit through the form. You may also add your GitHub username if you want to.

How we use it

We use this information to tell you when the source code is publicly released. We may send one follow-up message to correct the release link if needed. We never use your GitHub username to gather anything else from your profile.

Legal basis & storage

We process this information based on your consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time by asking us to delete your data. Google Forms collects and stores the submissions. Google may hold the data on servers in the United States or other countries. Only the project members who handle the release notification can see the responses.

Your choices & rights

Giving us your information is voluntary. Without an email address you will not receive the release notification. You can ask us to view, correct or delete your information at any time.

Contact

Questions or requests can be sent to:
CAP Lab, Department of Computer Engineering, Seoul National University
Email: cap@iris.snu.ac.kr
